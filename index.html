ForestVis
Lower Teakettle (NEON TEAK)

ForestVis is an interactive visualisation of forest data derived from LiDAR and multispectral imagery: explore the 3D point cloud alongside a linked minimap, filter trees by their attributes, and compare them in a UMAP projection.
